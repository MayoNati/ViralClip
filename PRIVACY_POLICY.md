# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 21, 2026**

## Introduction

ViralClip ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our desktop video editor application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide

When you use ViralClip, we may collect:
- Videos and media files you choose to edit
- Settings and preferences you configure within the Application
- TikTok account information (username, profile picture) when you connect your account

### 2. TikTok API Data

Through the TikTok API integration, we access:

#### user.info.basic
- Username
- Display name
- Profile picture
- Basic public profile information

#### video.upload
- Ability to upload videos to your TikTok account
- Video metadata (title, description, privacy settings you specify)

## How We Store Your Data

### Local Storage Only

**All your data is stored locally on your device.** We do not:
- Upload your videos to our servers
- Store your content in the cloud
- Maintain copies of your personal information on external servers

Your data remains on your computer, giving you full control over your content and privacy.

### Data Location

- Video files: Stored in your local application directory
- Application settings: Stored in local configuration files
- TikTok credentials: Managed securely through TikTok's OAuth authentication (tokens stored locally)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

1. **Provide Video Editing Services**: Process and edit your videos locally on your device
2. **Enable TikTok Integration**: Upload your edited videos to your TikTok account using the TikTok API
3. **Improve User Experience**: Remember your preferences and settings
4. **Application Functionality**: Ensure the Application works properly

## Data Sharing and Sale

### We Do Not Sell Your Data

**We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.** Your privacy is paramount to us.

### Third-Party Services

The only third-party service we integrate with is TikTok:

- **TikTok API**: When you upload a video, it is sent directly to TikTok's servers through their official API
- **TikTok's Privacy**: Your use of TikTok is governed by [TikTok's Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- We do not control or have access to how TikTok processes your data after upload

### No Other Third Parties

We do not share your information with:
- Advertisers
- Marketing companies
- Analytics services
- Data brokers
- Any other third parties (except as required by law)

## TikTok API Scopes and Permissions

We request the following TikTok API permissions:

### 1. user.info.basic
**Purpose**: To display your TikTok profile information in the Application
**Data Accessed**: 
- Username
- Display name
- Profile picture
- Public profile information

**Why We Need It**: To verify your identity and provide a personalized experience when managing your TikTok uploads.

### 2. video.upload
**Purpose**: To upload your edited videos to TikTok
**Data Accessed**: 
- Permission to upload video files to your TikTok account
- Video metadata you provide (title, description, privacy settings)

**Why We Need It**: This is the core functionality of ViralClip - to enable you to upload edited videos directly to TikTok without leaving the Application.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

- **Local Encryption**: Sensitive data stored locally may be encrypted
- **Secure Authentication**: TikTok credentials are managed through OAuth 2.0
- **No Server Storage**: Since we don't use external servers, your data isn't vulnerable to server breaches
- **Regular Updates**: We maintain the Application with security patches

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

- **Video Files**: Retained on your device until you delete them
- **Settings**: Retained until you uninstall the Application or clear application data
- **TikTok Tokens**: Retained until you disconnect your TikTok account or revoke access

You have full control to delete any data at any time by:
- Deleting video files from your local storage
- Clearing application data
- Uninstalling the Application

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

1. **Access Your Data**: All your data is stored locally and accessible to you at any time
2. **Delete Your Data**: Remove any data by deleting files or uninstalling the Application
3. **Disconnect TikTok**: Revoke TikTok API access at any time through TikTok's settings or our Application
4. **Export Your Data**: Copy your video files and content from your local storage at any time
5. **Opt-Out**: Stop using the Application at any time

## Children's Privacy

ViralClip is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.

## International Users

ViralClip is a desktop application that stores data locally. However, when you use the TikTok integration, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries where TikTok operates. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about their international data transfers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy
- Providing a notice in the Application (for material changes)

Your continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to Know**: You have the right to know what personal information we collect (all stored locally)
- **Right to Delete**: You have the right to request deletion of your information (you can do this directly by deleting files)
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale**: We do not sell your personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of
